Privacy PolicySubscription Terms

Sgt. Snooze is published by Grumblebox. Core alarm features and the Sergeant are free. An active Snooze Crew subscription lets you assign premium characters to alarms. The purchase screen displays the store's total price and billing period before you confirm.

Payment is charged through Apple App Store or Google Play. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through the originating store before renewal. Use Manage Subscription to change or cancel your plan. Cancellation takes effect according to your store's subscription terms; cancelling does not by itself refund a payment. Refund requests are handled by the originating store.

An existing alarm retains its selected premium character after subscription expiry or refund, including when you edit its time, days, challenge, or enabled state. Selecting a different premium character or assigning one to a new alarm requires an active subscription. Saving a switch to Sergeant or deleting the alarm removes that alarm's retained selection. Bundled updates to the same character remain available.

There is no Sgt. Snooze account or cross-store purchase transfer. Restore Purchases restores eligible subscriptions from the same Apple or Google store account. Alarms and retained selections are stored locally; uninstalling or clearing app data can remove them. Subscription restore does not restore your alarms.

Voice performances are AI-generated. New bundled content arrives through app updates; no particular release frequency is promised. Follow the app's setup guidance and check device alarm permissions. Delivery depends on operating-system capabilities and settings. Your statutory consumer rights are unaffected.
